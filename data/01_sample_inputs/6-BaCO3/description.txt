sample description: Polycrystalline 12R-BaFeO₃ was synthesized from BaCO₃ and Fe₂O₃ via solid-state reaction at 1273 K for 12 h under flowing oxygen, followed by oxidation with KClO₄ in a sealed platinum capsule at 2–3 GPa and 1273 K for 30 min, quenching to room temperature, and washing with distilled water.
Instrument description: Time-of-flight neutron powder diffraction data were collected using the TREND diffractometer at the China Spallation Neutron Source (CSNS), Dongguan, China, and the detector bank centered at 2θ=125.0° with a total flight path of 79.259 m was used for the present analysis.
