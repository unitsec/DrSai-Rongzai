Sample description:
Single-crystalline LiNi₀.₉₀Co₀.₀₅Mn₀.₀₅O₂ (NCM90-S) was synthesized through a long-duration LiOH/NaCl molten-salt route, which produced micrometer-sized polyhedral particles with an in situ formed rock-salt surface layer.

Instrument description:
Time-of-flight neutron powder diffraction data were collected using the TREND diffractometer at the China Spallation Neutron Source (CSNS), Dongguan, China, and the backscattering detector bank centered at 2θ=177.0∘ with a total flight path of 80.0027 m was used for the present analysis.
